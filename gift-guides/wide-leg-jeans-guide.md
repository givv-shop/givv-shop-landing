## How do I choose between the three pairs?

Choose by the mood you want: Marni for a clean, polished line, Toteme for a minimal everyday pair, Diesel for a relaxed washed look.

## How do I care for 100% cotton jeans?

Machine wash cold, as the Toteme and Diesel listings state.

Turning jeans inside out before washing and letting them dry flat helps keep the colour and shape. The HILP sweatshirts are machine washable.
