How to Style Wide Leg Jeans

A GIVV-SHOP buying guide

Wide leg jeans are the easiest way to look put-together without trying too hard. They balance a soft top, sit well over a loafer or a boot, and move from weekday to weekend with one change of shoe. Here is how we style the three pairs in our edit, and what to pair them with.

Which wide leg jeans does GIVV-SHOP carry?

We carry three pairs: Marni, Toteme and Diesel, each 100% cotton.

  • Marni Women Denim Wide Leg Jeans, $483. Woven cotton denim, made in Italy, in Italian sizes IT 38, 40 and 42 (IT 38 = US 2, IT 40 = US 4, IT 42 = US 6).
  • Toteme Women Wide Leg Denim, $395. Five-pocket, mid rise, with a top-button and zip fly and a rear logo patch. Made in Italy. Currently available in size 28 only.
  • Diesel Women 1996 D-Sire Jeans, $238. A washed finish, mid rise, with a top-button and concealed fly. Sizes 25 to 29.

How do I choose between the three pairs?

Choose by the mood you want: Marni for a clean, polished line, Toteme for a minimal everyday pair, Diesel for a relaxed washed look.

The Marni pair is the most considered of the three, and it suits tailored pieces and a sleek loafer. The Toteme is the quiet one: a simple five-pocket shape that works with almost anything in your wardrobe. The Diesel 1996 D-Sire has a washed finish that feels more casual, so it sits naturally with a sweatshirt and boots.

What top should I wear with wide leg jeans?

A soft, substantial sweatshirt is the simplest match, because its relaxed shape balances the volume of the leg.

Our HILP sweatshirts are made from combed cotton with a brushed interior (92% cotton, 8% lycra, 260 g/m²), in a regular, unisex fit, and made to order:

  • Unisex Cheetah Sweatshirt, $69, in black.
  • Unisex Grumpy Cat Sweatshirt, $69, in red, black or yellow.

For a cleaner line, tuck just the front of the sweatshirt into the waistband. For a softer, off-duty look, leave it loose over the Diesel or Toteme pair. The black Cheetah sweatshirt with any of the three jeans is the quietest combination in the edit.

Which shoes work best with wide leg jeans?

A loafer keeps the outfit polished, and a sturdy boot makes it relaxed; both work because the wide hem falls cleanly over them.

Loafers:

  • Tod's Black Leather Loafers Women, $675. Shiny leather with tone-on-tone stitching and a 2 cm sole.
  • Prada Women Brushed Fumé Leather Loafers, $780. Calf leather with a 2 cm heel.
  • Givenchy Women Moccasin "4G", $580. 100% lamb leather, made in Italy.

Boots:

  • 511 Black by Samelin, $176.40. Unisex boots with a water-repellent natural leather upper, TR rubber outsole and a lightweight textile lining. Made in Estonia.

Let the hem rest on the top of the shoe. With a loafer, a little of the shoe should show; with the 511 boot, the denim can fall over the shaft.

What are three easy outfits from this edit?

Here are three complete looks, each built from pieces on GIVV-SHOP:

1. Weekday polish: Marni wide leg jeans, black Cheetah sweatshirt half-tucked, Tod's black leather loafers.

2. Quiet weekend: Toteme wide leg denim, Grumpy Cat sweatshirt worn loose, Givenchy "4G" moccasin.

3. Cool-weather casual: Diesel 1996 D-Sire jeans, Cheetah sweatshirt, 511 Black boots.

Keep the colours calm (black, ivory, washed blue) and let one piece, such as a printed sweatshirt or a brushed leather loafer, carry the interest.

How do I care for 100% cotton jeans?

Machine wash cold, as the Toteme and Diesel listings state.

Turning jeans inside out before washing and letting them dry flat helps keep the colour and shape. The HILP sweatshirts are machine washable.
