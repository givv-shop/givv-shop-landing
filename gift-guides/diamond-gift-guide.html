Choosing a Diamond Gift

A GIVV-SHOP buying guide

A diamond gift should feel personal, last for years and be easy to wear. Every diamond piece at GIVV-SHOP is lab-grown and set in 14K gold or platinum. This guide walks through how to choose between a ring, a pendant and a bracelet, and what to check before you buy.

What kind of diamonds does GIVV-SHOP sell?

GIVV-SHOP sells lab-grown diamonds set in 14K gold or PT950 platinum.

As our oval ring listing puts it, a lab-grown diamond has the same optical properties as a mined diamond of the same grade; the difference is origin and price. Most of our pieces are AGI certified, and each listing states its colour and clarity grade.

Should I choose a ring, a pendant or a bracelet?

Choose a pendant or bracelet if you don't know her ring size; choose a ring when you can confirm the size first.

A pendant or a tennis bracelet needs no sizing, so it's the safest surprise. Rings come in sizes 5 to 8.5, and our listings advise going up a half size if she is between sizes and writing to us first. Resized, engraved and custom pieces are final sale, so it pays to check the size before ordering.

White gold, yellow gold or platinum?

Pick the metal she already wears most.

White gold and platinum give a cool, bright setting that suits a white stone. Yellow gold, offered on both pendants and the tennis bracelet, feels warmer and pairs well with gold jewelry she owns. Platinum, used on the Emerald Trinity Ring, does not fade and is hypoallergenic.
